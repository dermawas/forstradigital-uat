The actual problem
Expense tracking doesn't fail because you don't care about money

It fails because entering expenses is tedious. Logging a single coffee purchase is easy. Logging a supermarket receipt with 40 individual items isn't — so most people either dump the whole thing into one vague category, or just stop tracking altogether. exists to remove that specific friction, not to be "another budgeting app."

Honest about the AI: it does the first ~90% of the work — extracting items and guessing categories. You review and adjust before anything saves. It doesn't learn your preferences or "self-improve" over time; it's a well-scoped assistant, not autopilot.
