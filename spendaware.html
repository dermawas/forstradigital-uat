What it does
Two ways in, one place it all lands
Manual

Wallets, categories, budgets

Multiple wallets (cash, bank, credit card, e-wallet), custom categories, and monthly budgets with a spend breakdown by week, month, or year.

AI-assisted

Scan a receipt

Photograph a receipt and a backend-hosted AI splits it into line items with suggested categories — including multi-item receipts, split correctly across categories. A limited number of scans are free each day, supported by ads; a paid tier with a larger allowance is planned.

Optional

GnuCash Sync

Point the app at your own self-hosted PostgREST layer in front of your GnuCash PostgreSQL book, and expenses sync over as real, balanced transactions — indistinguishable from ones you entered in GnuCash desktop yourself.

Your data

You control the ledger

never connects to your GnuCash database directly, never sees your real database credentials, and can only ever insert new expenses through one narrowly-scoped function — never read balances or existing history, never alter your schema.

Who this beta is for
Comfortable installing an APK directly

This is aimed at people already comfortable self-hosting things — GnuCash users especially, since GnuCash Sync assumes you're willing to run a small PostgREST service yourself. If that's not you yet, the app works perfectly well standalone with no GnuCash setup at all.

  • You're fine sideloading an APK and allowing "install unknown apps" once
  • You optionally already run (or are willing to run) GnuCash against a PostgreSQL-backed book
  • You're OK with a beta that's still being actively shaped by feedback
